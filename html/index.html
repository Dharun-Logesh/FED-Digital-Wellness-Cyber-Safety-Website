Overcoming Modern Challenges

With technology becoming more apparent, it's easy to experience Digital Burnout and fall into Cyber Insecurity. Therefore, it's important that we learn to overcome these challenges and harness the full benefits of technology!

Problems and Benefits of technology

  • Digital burnout & eye strain
  • Cyberbullying & online threats
  • Privacy concerns
  • Reduced physical activity

Privacy Concerns 🔐

  • Identity Theft
  • Financial loss
  • Less trust in digital platforms
  • Loss of freedom

Cyberbullying 💔

  • Victims suffer emotional trauma
  • Decreased self-esteem
  • Social withdrawl
  • Sometimes, suicidal thoughts

Ben du Lipenson
@itsbendu

For the longest time, I didn't realize how much time I was wasting scrolling through social media every day. It became a habit that left me feeling tired and distracted. After learning about digital wellness, I started setting daily screen time limits and scheduling breaks away from my phone. The change was amazing—I felt more focused in school, less anxious, and even had more time for hobbies I love.

Ali Mandipadi
@ali_going_live

I remember someone I thought was a friend online asked me to share my password, and I almost did because I trusted them. Luckily, I took a cyber safety workshop before anything serious happened. It really opened my eyes to how careful you need to be about what you share and who you trust online. Now, I'm much more cautious and always double-check before sharing any personal info.

Christopher Cathedra
@your_friendly_chris

Being bullied online felt overwhelming and isolating at first. I wasn't sure how to handle it until I talked to my parents and teachers about it. They helped me learn how to block and report bullies, and it gave me back control over my online world. That experience made me realize the importance of having strong cyber safety habits and a support system you can rely on.
